02 / Sample experience

What does a lesson actually look like?

Here's an illustrative learning experience — not an advertised scheduled event.

A practical money decision

You have M500. What should you do with it?

Rather than immediately telling learners to buy a share, the lesson begins with their financial situation.

1
Check readiness.

Do you need this money for essentials or emergencies?

2
Compare options.

What changes if you save it, buy shares or explore bonds?

3
Understand risks.

What happens if the share price falls, fees apply or you need your money quickly?

4
Check legitimacy.

How would you investigate the platform and identify scam warning signs?

Learner takeaway

A practical decision checklist — not a promise of investment returns.

04 / Common questions

Questions worth asking.

Do I need money to start learning?

No. You can learn how investments work before committing any money.

Is this only for people who already invest?

No. The learning pathways cover financial foundations, beginner investing and more advanced comparisons.

Will I be told which shares or cryptocurrencies to buy?

No. The initiative focuses on education, risk awareness and independent decision-making, not personalised investment recommendations.

Will I learn about South African platforms such as EasyEquities?

Practical examples may include South African investment platforms. Eligibility, costs, funding routes and legal requirements must be verified before any real transaction.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Investments can lose value, and cryptocurrency can be especially volatile. Understanding potential losses is part of the learning experience.
